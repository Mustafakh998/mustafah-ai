# MUSTAFAH-AI — BAGHDAD EDITION
## Operating Cost Analysis & Budget Control Guide

---

## 1. Core Financial Constraint

* **Monthly Advertising Budget:** Strictly **< $100 / month**.
* **Software Subscriptions:** Must be decoupled from the $100 advertising budget; prioritize free and low-cost tiers.
* **Goal:** Maximum patient conversion at the lowest possible cost per attended patient (CAC).

│ Patient Acquisition  │      │ Video Retargeting    │      │ Testing & Tuning     │
│ (Baghdad Ortho Reel) │      │ (75% Reel Viewers)   │      │ (E-max / Pediatric)  │

### Stream 1: Main Patient Acquisition Campaign ($60 / month)
* **Target:** Baghdad geographic radius (Zayouna, Mansour, Karrada, Jadriya; ages 18–35).
* **Creative:** Dentist-led original video (Dr. Mustafa Hadi discussing Open Bite / Damon system).
* **Objective:** Click-to-Instagram Direct.
* **Expected Metrics:** ~18,000 impressions, 35–45 conversations, 20–25 qualified leads.

### Stream 2: Retargeting High-Intent Viewers ($20 / month)
* **Target:** Custom audience of Baghdad users who watched 75% or more of orthodontic educational reels.
* **Creative:** Testimonial & Before/After case alignment with clear appointment CTA.
* **Expected Metrics:** ~5,000 impressions, 15 conversations, 10–12 qualified inquiries.

### Stream 3: Testing & Seasonal Reserve ($20 / month)
* **Target:** Alternating tests between E-max Cosmetic Smiles (2-session guarantee) and Pediatric gentle care (SDF painless visits).
* **Control:** Only released upon explicit clinic administrator sign-off.

## 4. Acquisition Unit Economics & ROI Projection

* **Average Case Value (Orthodontics in Baghdad):** 1,200,000 – 1,800,000 IQD (~$800 – $1,200 USD).
* **Monthly Attended Patients from $80–$100 Ad Spend:** 8 – 14 attended patients.
* **Projected New Monthly Treatment Revenue:** ~$6,400 – $12,000 USD.
* **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** **60x – 120x**, turning a modest sub-$100 budget into significant clinical cashflow.
